Microsoft Research

Xun Wu （吴浔）

Email: xunwu [at] microsoft.com

I am a researcher at Microsoft Research. My research focuses on post-training for large language models, mixture-of-experts, and low-bit language models. I am broadly interested in building frontier foundation models.

I received my master's degree in Computer Software Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2024.
